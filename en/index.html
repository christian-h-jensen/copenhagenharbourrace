Copenhagen Harbour Race welcomes you back 10th October 2026.

Copenhagen Harbour Race is an annual international regatta for 8+, Inrigger, and coastal boats in the heart of the Copenhagen Harbour. It is one of the biggest rowing events in Copenhagen with more than 400 participants.
