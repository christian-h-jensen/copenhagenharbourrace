Dinner and Party

After the regatta, there will as usual be an opportunity to join us for dinner and the after (season) party at Københavns Roklub. To participate in the dinner and party, you must purchase a ticket via Billetto no later than 5 October:

Purchase Harbour Race dinner ticket

Please remember to include the name of your rowing club together with your name when registering, so that you can be seated together with club/team mates during dinner.

Vegetarian food is only available if you send an email to majacaroe@hotmail.com requesting it.

If you are only attending the party, the doors open at 21:30 and there is an entrance fee of DKK 60, payable upon arrival.

Programme:

18:00 – Doors open

18:30 – Dinner

21:30 – Party with DJ

The menu will be a communal Nordic-style dinner with Nordic tacos and build-your-own flatbreads, followed by ice cream for dessert.

Københavns Roklub is cashless, and payment is possible via MobilePay or PayPal.

The number of dinner guests is limited to 150 pax. Registration is based on first come, first served basis. Only full payment is a binding registration.

Entry fee to the party only is 60 DKK at the door. Party opens at 9:30 PM. No registration required.
