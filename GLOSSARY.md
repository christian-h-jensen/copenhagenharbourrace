# Copenhagen Harbour Race

An annual international rowing regatta for eights, inriggers and coastal boats in Copenhagen's harbour, organised by Københavns Roklub and Bryggens Roklub.

## Event

**Copenhagen Harbour Race**:
The regatta and the site's name. Spelled "Harbour" (British), matching the domain.
_Avoid_: Harbor Race (except where it appears on the course map image)

**Course** (DA: *Banen*):
The marked route in the South Harbour from start/finish near Langebro to the turn at Slusen and back.
_Avoid_: Route, track

**Race class** (DA: *Løbsklasse*):
A category crews compete in, defined by boat type and crew (e.g. 8+ Open men, Inrigger Mix).
_Avoid_: Category, division

**Party** (DA: *Fest*):
The dinner and after-party at Københavns Roklub following the regatta.
_Avoid_: Dinner, event

**Results**:
The official times per race class, published on the DFfR registration system (tilmeld.roning.dk), not on this site.
