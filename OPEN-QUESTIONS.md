# Open questions about the content

Things that don't match between pages or languages. Ask the organisers before changing them, then update both `da/` and `en/`.

## 1. Party-only door time (Danish party page)

The old Danish text said two different times for the party-only entrance:

- "Hvis du udelukkende deltager i festen, åbner dørene kl. 21.30 …"
- "Dørene åbnes til festen kl. 21:20."

The two sentences are now merged into one box on `da/party.html`, using **21:30** to match the first sentence, the English page and the front page programme. Confirm 21:30 is right.

## 2. Where do inriggers and coastal boats launch?

- `classes.html` (DA and EN): showers, parking and launching at **SAS Rowing Club** (Roklubben SAS). On race day the lawn and pier at ARK can be used as well.
- `da/rules.html`, inrigger/coastal rule 11: boats **must** be launched from **Bryggens Roklub or Amager Ro- og Kajakklub (ARK)**, or the crew is turned away.
- `addresses.html` lists Københavns Roklub, Bryggens Roklub and ARK, but not SAS. The address map (`img/addresses-map-*.png`) shows "Roklubben SAS" right next to ARK.

Is "SAS Rowing Club" the old name for Bryggens Roklub? If so, the classes pages (and possibly the address map) should say Bryggens Roklub.

## 3. Three shallow areas, but only two photos

`course.html` (DA and EN) says there are **three** very shallow areas, but only two photos are shown: Skudehavnen and Tømmergraven. Either a photo of the third area is missing, or the text should say two.

## 4. English rules are missing the inrigger and coastal section

`da/rules.html` has a whole section, "Regattaregler for coastal- og inriggerløbene" (11 rules: course, start, overtaking buoy, boat requirements, crew, timing, handicap, seeding, clothing, registration, launching). `en/rules.html` only has the 8+ rules and the Best Club rules. The section needs translating for the English page.
